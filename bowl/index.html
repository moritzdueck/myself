Nutrition: Max Rubner-Institut (2025): Bundeslebensmittelschlüssel (BLS), Version 4.0 – Deutsche Nährstoffdatenbank. Karlsruhe. DOI: 10.25826/Data20251217-134202-0 (CC BY 4.0). Amounts are raw weight, before cooking; cooked values are the BLS cooked food, scaled by its yield. Duck breast and corn are weighed as eaten. Foods BLS lacks use a close one's values, noted on each.

Fibre: at least 14 g per 1000 kcal (US Dietary Reference Intakes, 2005), in line with the German Nutrition Society's (DGE) 30 g a day.

Saturated fat and free sugars: at most 10% of the energy each (WHO; DGE). Up to a fifth past a limit, or short of the fibre minimum, is "slightly"; that margin is ours, not a guideline's. Free sugars are those in honey, syrups, juices and balsamic vinegar, plus added sugar, estimated against the plain food (canned fruit against fresh; dried cranberries against fresh, from USDA). Sugar in whole fruit, vegetables and milk isn't free.

Ideas: how often foods share a recipe, across 77,000 bowl and salad recipes in English and German (Web Data Commons, schema.org extraction 2024-12, from the October 2024 Common Crawl).

Default amounts are estimates, not measured portions.
