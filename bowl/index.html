Nutrition: Max Rubner-Institut (2025): Bundeslebensmittelschlüssel (BLS), Version 4.0 – Deutsche Nährstoffdatenbank. Karlsruhe. DOI: 10.25826/Data20251217-134202-0 (CC BY 4.0). Amounts are raw or dry weight, what you buy and weigh before cooking; cooked values come from the BLS cooked food, scaled by its yield against the raw one (energy is kept through cooking without fat). Duck breast and corn have no raw counterpart in BLS and are weighed as eaten. BLS has no black beans, salsa or poultry mince: they use the values of kidney beans, raw tomato and chicken thigh.

Fibre: at least 14 g per 1000 kcal of the meal, the energy-based adequate intake from the US Dietary Reference Intakes (Institute of Medicine, 2005); it matches the German Nutrition Society's (DGE) reference of at least 30 g a day at about 2100 kcal.

Ideas: how often foods share a recipe, over 77,000 bowl and salad recipes in English and German from Web Data Commons, schema.org extraction 2024-12 (recipes marked up on pages of the October 2024 Common Crawl).

Default gram amounts are prototype estimates, not measured portions.
